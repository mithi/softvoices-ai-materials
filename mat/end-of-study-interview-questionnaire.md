# ♥️ Interview Guide (~15 min, recorded)

Fixed core questions — follow interesting answers with probes.

**Warm-up**
- How was the overall experience?
- In your own words, what were the AI agents doing?

**A moment it worked**
- Tell me about a time a comment changed how you saw a post.
  - Probe: What did it say? What shifted? Would you have gotten there alone?

**A moment it didn't**
- Tell me about a comment that didn't land, or that you ignored. Why?

**Tone**
- The agents acknowledged your point before adding anything, and stayed tentative. Notice that?
  - Probe: How did it compare to normal online replies — open you up, or feel soft/evasive?

**Trust**
- Did you trust the agents? Did you catch any being wrong — how did that affect the rest?

**Change**
- Has how you read or react to social media changed at all, even slightly? An example from outside the study?

**Wrap**
- What annoyed you most?
- One thing you'd change?
- Anything I should've asked but didn't?
