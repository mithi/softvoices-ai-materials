### Trust in the agents

_1 = Not at all, 7 = Completely._

- I trusted the agents' factual claims. `(1–7)`
- I noticed the agents making mistakes. `(1–7)`
- Describe any comment you flagged, and why. `[open text]`
  > These two items measure **different things** (trust vs. catching errors). Keep them as two separate scores. Do not average them, and do not reverse-score — reverse-scoring only makes sense inside a multi-item scale, and there's none here.
