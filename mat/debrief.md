
# Debrief (after Session 7)

Thanks for taking part. Here's what this was really about.

We tested SoftVoicesAI — AI agents that comment on a post and on your reply. Each agent had a job: point out a hidden assumption, add a missing angle, or flag weak reasoning. All written to sound calm and acknowledging, not argumentative.

## Our question

After reading the comments, does your second response show more complex thinking — weighing more than one side? We look at how you wrote, not whether your opinion was "right." There was no correct answer.


## One thing to know

Some AI comments had mistakes on purpose. We didn't fact-check them first — we wanted to see what real users catch. Don't take any AI claim from these sessions as true without checking it yourself.
