## What this study is about
We're testing an AI tool that comments on social media posts and on your written replies. We want to learn how people think and feel while using it.

## What you'll do
- 8 short sessions (~10 - 20 minutes) over about [X] weeks.
- Session 0: sign this form, answer background questions, do a practice round.
- Sessions 1–6: read a post, write your view, read AI comments, write your view again (include changes if any), answer a few quick questions.
- Session 7: longer questionnaire + recorded interview.

## Please know

- AI can be wrong. Treat its comments like any stranger's.
- Posts are real and chosen to spark strong reactions. 
- They won't cover race, gender, or sexual orientation.
- There's no "correct" answer we want you to reach. We just want to see how you think.

## What we record
- Your written answers
- your reactions/flags on comments
- questionnaire answers
- an audio recording of the final interview (voice masked)

## Your data and privacy

- Results reported as group data. Any quotes are anonymized.
- Anonymized data may be shared publicly for open science — nothing that identifies you.
