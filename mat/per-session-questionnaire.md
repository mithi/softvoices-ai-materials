Per-Session Questionnaire (after each session)

 1. **Mental effort:** *"The mental effort I invested in this post and comments was:"*
2. **Productive effort — germane:** *"The effort I put in went into thinking that felt worthwhile."*
3. **Wasted effort — extraneous:** *"I spent effort on confusion or clutter, not the topic itself."*
4. **Effectiveness:** *"Reading the comments helped me think more carefully about the post."*
5. **Enjoyment:** *"I enjoyed this session."*
6. **Willingness to adopt:** *"If this tool existed, I would use it on posts I come across."*

**Scale key**
- **Item 1** — Paas mental effort: **1–9** (1 = very, very low … 9 = very, very high)
- **Items 2–6** — **1–7**, disagree → agree
